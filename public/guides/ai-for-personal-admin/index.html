The prompt · works in every tool
Read this [bill / form / booking]. Use only what is written in it.
1. What is it for, in one plain sentence?
2. The total, exactly as printed, and whether tax is already included.
3. The due date, exactly as printed. If only payment terms are printed (like "within 15 days"), quote the terms, then give the date they point to and mark it WORKED OUT. If nothing is printed, write NOT PRINTED.
4. Check the maths: do the lines add up to the subtotal, and the subtotal plus tax to the total? Show the sums.
5. Anything unclear, missing or unusual. If you cannot read a number, say so instead of guessing.
Dates: if a date could be read two ways, say so, and use the billing period or month names on the bill to decide. This supplier is in [Canada / the US].

Where you see [ ], keep the one word that fits and delete the rest.

For a form, ask what each box means and which official page sets the number. For a trip, ask for a list of every price, date and entry rule to confirm on the official site.

Your ID numbers, your money details and your passwords.
